Affiliate Compliance Guidelines

You must follow these rules when promoting ShuSpot. Violations may result in account termination.

RULE 1: NEVER Call ShuSpot a Tutoring Service
ShuSpot is a parent-supervised English reading and speaking platform. Due to China's regulatory environment, you must never describe ShuSpot as: a tutoring service, an English school, a cram school, an off-campus training program, or a replacement for tutoring.
✓ DO SAY:
ShuSpot is a reading platform for families
A parent-supervised English reading tool
An English reading and speaking practice library
✗ DON'T SAY:
ShuSpot replaces your English tutor
Better than cram school
Online English tutoring platform
RULE 2: NEVER Say 'ShuSpot.com' in Videos
On all platforms, say "ShuSpot" or "Search ShuSpot" only. Showing .com URLs in video content gets flagged by platform algorithms and reduces your reach.
RULE 3: No Guaranteed Learning Outcomes
Do not promise specific results like "Your child will be fluent in 3 months" or "Guaranteed reading improvement." You can share personal experience and general benefits.
RULE 4: Platform-Specific Link Rules
Follow these guidelines for each platform:
XiaoHongShu
No direct links in posts. Use keyword comments (e.g., "Comment READ for details") and respond via DM. Link in bio is OK.
Douyin
No external links in videos. Use profile link and keyword comments.
Bilibili
Link in video description is usually OK. Never in the video itself.
WeChat
Links work naturally in articles and menu buttons.
RULE 5: Content Ratio Rule
3 out of every 5 posts should be pure value (parenting tips, reading advice, challenges) with NO product push. Only 2 out of 5 should showcase ShuSpot. Flipping this ratio causes follower drops and algorithm punishment.
RULE 6: Honest Recommendations Only
Only promote ShuSpot if you genuinely believe in the product. Your followers trust you. Dishonest promotion damages your credibility and ours. If you haven't tried ShuSpot with a child, do so before promoting it.

Frequently Asked Questions
When do I get paid? ▼
Payouts are processed monthly on the 1st for the previous month's earnings. Minimum threshold: ¥200.
How long does the tracking cookie last? ▼
90 days. If someone clicks your link and subscribes within 90 days, you get credit.
Can I promote on multiple platforms? ▼
Yes! We encourage it. Use the same referral link across all your platforms.
What if my follower wants a refund? ▼
If a subscription is refunded within 7 days, the commission is reversed. This is shown in your earnings breakdown.
How do I change my payment method? ▼
Go to Account Settings and update your payment preferences. Changes take effect on the next payout.
